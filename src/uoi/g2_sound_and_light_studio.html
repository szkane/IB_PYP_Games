Why does a mouse squeak so high?

Tiny vocal cords vibrate super-duper FAST! Fast vibrations make a High Pitch sound wave with tight ripples.

🌍 We hear this in: Whistles, baby birds, and squeaky sneakers!

Why do ambulances flash lights and wail?

Sirens jump high and low fast so our ears notice right away! Flashing red and blue lights tell all cars to pull over safely.

🌍 Why we use it: Emergency warnings to clear the road fast!
