Why does a mouse sound like that?

Tiny vocal cords vibrate super-duper FAST! Fast vibrations make a High Pitch sound wave with tight ripples.

🌍 When do we hear this? Referee whistles, chirping baby birds, squeaky sneakers on gym floors!

Why do ambulances flash and wail?

High-low wails jump between pitches so human brains instantly pay attention! Flashing lights show cars where the ambulance is even from far away!

🌍 Purpose: Communicating an urgent emergency message without using any spoken words!
