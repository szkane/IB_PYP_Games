Key Concepts · Form & Connection A digraph is two letters teaming up to make one special sound (sh, ch, th, wh, ph, ck, ng, nk). Recognizing digraphs helps us decode and spell new words smoothly!
🚀 Take Action in Real Life Open a storybook today and hunt for 5 words with digraphs like "sh", "ch", "th", "ng", or "nk". Read them out loud to someone!
