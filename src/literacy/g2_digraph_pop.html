🌟 IB PYP Reflection & Action

Key Concept · Form & Expression A digraph is two letters teaming up to make one special sound (like sh, ch, th, wh, ph, ck, ng, nk). Knowing digraphs helps us read and spell confidently!
🚀 Take Action in Real Life Open a storybook today and hunt for 5 words with digraphs like "sh", "ch", "th", or "ck". Read them out loud to someone!
