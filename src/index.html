CaringPrincipledResearch SkillsSelf-management Skills

◎UOI

Needs of Living Things Give plants and animals what they need and explain why. UOI4 Living Things - Eco Detective Mission Investigate living/non-living things, needs, and protection choices.

123Math

Story Journey: Pictograph and Tally Adventure Collect and read data using pictographs and tally charts. Quiz Arcade: Pictograph and Tally Sprint Fast data questions about how many, most, and least.

AaLiteracy

⚗Science

Bike Gear Lab Observe cause and effect in a simple interactive system.

文Chinese 中文

Unit 5 · How the World Works

Patterns and Cycles

People adapt their daily life and practices to nature's patterns and cycles.

InquirerKnowledgeableResearch SkillsThinking Skills

◎UOI

Life Cycle Builder Arrange nature cycle stages and notice what changes next.

⚗Science

Day and Night Detectives Investigate day/night changes and evidence. Moon Phases Flashcards Recognize moon phases as a natural cycle. Three.js Solar System Explore planets, orbits, and repeated movement. NEON PLANETS - Particle Engine A space and motion extension for observing repeated movement. Ultimate Galaxy Engine Explore spiral motion and visual patterns in space. Crystal Galaxy Observe color, light, and repeated particle patterns. Milky Way Gesture Control Use movement to explore a modeled galaxy scene. Hand-Controlled Particle System Investigate how input changes a visual system. Blue Plasma Orb Interface Observe light, color, and motion patterns. Stable Hand Particle System Gesture-based particle motion extension. Random Color Hand Particles Experiment with changing color and movement.

123Math

Time Train - Before & After Sequence daily routines using before and after.

AaLiteracy

Spelling Bee for IB PYP G1 Practice time, cycle, and nature vocabulary.

文Chinese 中文

Pinyin Tone Helper Practice tones for time, season, and nature-cycle words. Chinese Practice Print Create handwriting practice for cycle and calendar vocabulary.

Unit 6 · Where We Are in Place and Time

Journeys

Personal journeys show how people learn, grow, and move forward.

Open-MindedReflectiveSelf-management SkillsCommunication Skills

◎UOI

Story Sequencer Sequence journey moments and explain how a person grows or changes. Goal Steps Quest Choose actions that help prepare for a new stage in a learning journey.

AaLiteracy

Time Train - Before & After Use time words to retell personal journeys in order. Spelling Bee for IB PYP G1 Practice growth, change, and personal narrative vocabulary. MoveSpell - Magic Hand Edition Blend, segment, and spell high-frequency words for personal writing. Pronunciation Studio Hear and repeat sounds before sharing personal journey stories aloud.

123Math

Position Explorer Describe journeys and views using in front of, behind, above, below, and next to. Arithmetic Worksheet Generator Review number bonds, place value, comparing numbers, operations, and word problems. Kangaroo Math G1 Review Grade 1 maths skills with short practice missions.

文Chinese 中文

Pinyin Tone Helper Practice clear tones for speaking about experiences and growth. Chinese Practice Print Create handwriting practice for personal journey and time vocabulary. Radical Spinner Build character awareness while preparing personal writing words.
